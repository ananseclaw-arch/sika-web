Shopify Payments in Africa What Works and What Does Not

Quick answer: Shopify Payments has a presence in Africa but faces adoption challenges due to limited transaction volume there compared to its dominant US market.
Published 2026-08-251 min readSourced & fact-checked
Quick summary
  • Shopify serves over 5 million merchants globally.
  • Shopify processes approximately US$292.3 billion in transactions annually.
  • The United States accounts for 57% of Shopify's processed transaction volume.

Shopify Inc. operates as a Canadian multinational corporation, specializing in e-commerce solutions. Its headquarters are located in Ottawa, Ontario. The company provides a cloud-based e-commerce management platform (CEMP) designed to power retail point-of-sale systems. This infrastructure supports businesses in managing their online stores and related operations effectively.

Shopify boasts a significant global customer base, reportedly serving over 5 million merchants. The company processes a vast amount of transactions annually, reportedly reaching US$292.3 billion. It's important to note that these figures represent the overall volume processed on its platform, encompassing sales from all types of merchants, large and small. A substantial portion of this volume originates from the United States, which is reportedly the company's largest market, accounting for 57% of its processed transactions.

The platform's versatility attracts a diverse range of major enterprise clients. These large-scale users include prominent brands from various sectors, such as Tesla (the electric vehicle company), LVMH (the luxury goods conglomerate), and food and beverage giants like Nestlé, PepsiCo, AB InBev, Kraft Heinz, Lindt, and Red Bull. Furthermore, significant supermarket chains including Whole Foods Market and Hyatt, the hospitality operator, also rely on Shopify's systems. This wide array of high-profile clients demonstrates the platform's capability to support complex retail operations across different industries and geographies.
